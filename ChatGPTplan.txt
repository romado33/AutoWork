[View the README](sandbox:/mnt/data/WorkRecorderAutomation/README.md)

It is built around **Vibe**, not noScribe. Current Vibe forwards command-line operations to its bundled Sona transcription engine, and Sona currently supports exactly the kind of local scripted transcription we need: `transcribe <model> <audio>`.  Vibe itself also documents local/offline transcription, batch operation, CLI support, and Ollama integration.

The completed workflow is:

```text
VOICE RECORDER
      │
      │ USB plugged into PC
      ▼
Windows watcher detects YOUR recorder
(by volume serial number)
      │
      ▼
COPY ALL NEW AUDIO TO PC
      │
      ├── recorder is no longer needed
      │
      ▼
VIBE / SONA
Local Whisper transcription
      │
      ▼
Individual transcripts
      │
      ▼
Combined chronological daily transcript
      │
      ▼
OLLAMA
Local AI analysis
      │
      ├── Tasks assigned to you
      ├── Things you promised to do
      ├── Requests made of you
      ├── Deadlines
      ├── Decisions
      ├── Important facts
      ├── People to follow up with
      ├── Open questions
      └── Possible forgotten follow-ups
      │
      ▼
Daily Summary - YYYY-MM-DD.md
      │
      ▼
EMAIL SUMMARY TO YOU
```

Ollama runs its API locally at `localhost:11434`, so the summarization portion does not require a paid AI API or send the transcript to an AI cloud service. ([Ollama][1])

### What to do now

1. Install **Vibe** and **Ollama** on your Windows PC if you haven't already.
2. Extract my ZIP.
3. Plug your recorder into the computer.
4. Double-click **`INSTALL.cmd`**.

The setup walks you through everything. It detects Vibe and Ollama, lets you identify which USB drive is your recorder, downloads the transcription model, downloads the local Ollama model, and optionally configures email.

For transcription you'll get three choices:

```text
1. base.en      fastest
2. small.en     recommended
3. medium.en    best accuracy of the three
```

I'd start with **small.en**. If recordings through clothing or across a room aren't transcribing accurately enough, we can later switch to `medium.en`.

For summaries I've defaulted to:

```text
gemma3:4b
```

which should be reasonably manageable on a normal Windows machine. We can choose a stronger model once I know your computer's RAM, CPU and GPU.

### Email is included

During installation it asks:

```text
Email each completed daily summary automatically? [Y/n]
```

It has presets for Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, Microsoft 365, or custom SMTP.

For Gmail, you'll use a Google **App Password**, not your normal Gmail password. The password is stored using Windows' built-in DPAPI encryption rather than sitting in `config.json` as plain text.

By default I deliberately configured it to email **only the AI summary**, not the entire transcript. You can optionally attach the full transcript, but that's disabled because the transcript is considerably more sensitive.

### I also accounted for failures

This isn't just a straight one-shot script. It tracks every recording it has already processed.

If something goes wrong:

```text
USB disconnected early
        → copied files remain locally

Vibe fails
        → transcription retried next time

Ollama isn't running
        → missing summary retried next time

Email server unavailable
        → unsent summary retried next time

Same recorder plugged in twice
        → already-processed audio is ignored

Same summary encountered again
        → email hash prevents duplicate delivery
```

Another improvement I made is that it **copies every new recording first**, before beginning the slower transcription. So Vibe is working against the PC's local copy rather than keeping your recorder busy for the whole transcription process.

### Long workdays are handled differently

A seven-hour workday transcript could be far too large to simply dump into an AI model. The workflow therefore uses hierarchical summarization:

```text
Full daily transcript
        ↓
~18,000-character chunks
        ↓
AI extracts memory/action information
from each chunk
        ↓
Partial summaries
        ↓
Deduplicate + merge
        ↓
Final daily summary
```

That should produce a much better "what do I need to remember from today?" report than simply asking an AI to summarize seven hours of transcript.
